1Overview

Dotvine (“the app”, “the game”) is a casual color-connect puzzle game. You draw paths between matching colored dots to fill the board. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything the game needs stays on your device.

2Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. In particular, the app does not collect or ask for:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details,
  • your location,
  • photos, camera or microphone data,
  • your contacts or files,
  • advertising identifiers or device identifiers,
  • usage analytics or crash reports.

The app has no servers and no user accounts, so we have no way of receiving your data even if we wanted to.

3Data stored on your device

To remember where you left off, the game saves a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • your game progress (the levels you have completed and unlocked), and
  • your settings (sound on/off, haptic feedback on/off and light/dark/auto theme).

This information never leaves your device through the app and is not linked to your identity.

4Permissions

The app works without requesting any special permissions. It does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or the internet. Sound effects and haptic feedback use your device’s standard system features and can be turned off at any time in Settings inside the app.

5Third parties, ads and analytics

The app does not include advertising, analytics, social-media or other third-party tracking tools, and it does not share information with any third party.

Good to know: Google Play and your device’s operating system are separate services with their own privacy practices (for example, when you download or update the app). We don’t control them and receive no data from them about you.

6Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no purchases and no external links inside gameplay.

7Data security and backups

Your progress and settings are kept in the app’s private storage area on your device, protected by Android’s app sandbox. Since no data is transmitted, there is nothing to intercept in transit.

Device backups: if you have Android’s backup feature enabled, your device may back up app data (such as your progress) to your own Google account. That is handled by Android under your own account settings; we cannot access it.

8Your choices and rights

You are in control of the data the game stores:

  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or clear its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Dotvine → Storage → Clear data. This removes your saved progress and settings.
  • Change settings: adjust sound, haptics and theme in the app’s Settings screen at any time.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. If you still have a privacy question or request, just contact us below.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a future version of the app adds a feature that handles data differently. The latest version will always be available on this page and the “Effective” date at the top will be updated. We will not add data collection without updating this policy first.
